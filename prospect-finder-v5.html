Prospect Finder v5

Built around one idea: your buyer is a firm with a small channel. A fund publishing weekly to 900 subscribers is spending money and getting nothing back — maximum pain, no incumbent agency, and the founder still reads their own messages. Big channels already have someone.

Keys
Niche

Short queries return far more. YouTube treats a long query as an AND across every word, so "pre-seed founder story silicon valley" returns almost nothing while "angel investing podcast" returns hundreds.

Filters

Most channels never set a country. Strict mode throws every one of those away — that is what emptied your last run.

Leave at 0 until you have enough rows, then raise to 45 to keep only firms.
